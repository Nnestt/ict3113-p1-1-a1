## 5. Why not qwen2.5:1.5b → phi3.5:3.8b

No τ makes a qwen2.5:1.5b → phi3.5:3.8b cascade pass on tuning (none found): phi3.5:3.8b alone already fails Credit reporting, and accepting qwen2.5:1.5b's answers cannot repair it. So the pair is qwen2.5:1.5b → qwen2.5:7b.

## 6. Why not a length rule (brief method 4)

## 7. Other methods

- **Agreement (method 2):** the only other small model, llama3.2:1b, is right on 18/88 (20.5%) of tuning tickets, so agreement with it carries little signal, and two small calls cost close to one large one.
- **Self-reported confidence (method 3):** needs a second prompt and a second model call on every ticket, and small models are often confidently wrong.
